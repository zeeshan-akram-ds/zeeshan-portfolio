The Profit Audit

One fixed-scope engagement. I work from an export of your orders and answer four questions.

  • Are your discounts buying growth or giving margin away?

    I compare discounted and full-price orders line by line, so the cost of each discount habit is visible.

  • Which few products carry your profit?

    And which ones earn almost nothing while causing delivery problems and clutter.

  • How much depends on a small group of customers?

    Where your best buyers are concentrated, and what you stand to lose if they leave.

  • Where would a stockout or slow shipping hurt most?

    Single products or shipping tiers carrying more of the business than anyone has planned for.

What you get

A plain-English report that ranks the findings by what each one costs you and says what to do about it, followed by a walkthrough call. Most audits take 2 to 8 business days from data access to final report. The fee is fixed and agreed on the first call, before any work starts.

How to read the numbers

Observed
Measured directly in the dataset.
Modeled
A what-if built from observed data, such as a 20% margin drop. It is not savings, recovered revenue or a forecast.
Synthetic
Microsoft’s Contoso retail data. It behaves like a real business but is not one.

Common questions

Is my business data safe with you?

I work from read-only exports or view-only access, never admin access to your store. An NDA is available and signed before any data is shared, and I delete the raw data when the engagement ends unless you ask me to keep it. The analysis does not need customer names or emails, so you can remove them from the export first.

What if the audit finds nothing meaningful?

I would say so plainly in the report and not pad it. The first call exists to check this: if your order history is too thin, or too much of your business runs through retailers, I will tell you before you pay for anything. In all four of my case-study datasets, profit was concentrated in a few products, discounts or customers, because standard dashboards report averages rather than concentration.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

You work directly with me from the first call to the final report, not with an account manager relaying findings from a team you never meet. That means no overhead markup and someone who understands your data rather than running a templated checklist.

How long does an audit take?

Most audits take 2 to 8 business days from data access to final report, depending on data volume and how clean it is. I confirm a specific timeline on the first call.

Do I need to be technical to understand the result?

No. You get a plain-English report and a walkthrough call. The report is a ranked list of findings, what each one costs you, and what to do about it. The technical work stays behind the scenes.

What size of business is a good fit?

Shopify and DTC brands doing roughly $500K to $20M a year. Pre-revenue stores do not have enough order history for the analysis to mean much, and brands that sell almost entirely through retailers do not own the order data an audit needs. Larger retailers usually have an in-house team for this work.

Which time zones do you work in?

I work remotely and hold calls in UK afternoon and US morning hours. Most of the work is asynchronous, so you do not need to be online while I run the audit.
